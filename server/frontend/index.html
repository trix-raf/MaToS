MaToS (Make Text on Sign) is a revolutionary software solution designed to act as a real-time bridge. By utilizing cutting-edge computer vision and machine learning, MaToS recognizes hand gestures, facial expressions, and body language, instantly converting them into readable text.

MaToS is designed to work with the camera you already have, with no extra hardware. Clearer video still gives clearer results, so good lighting and keeping your hands, face and upper body in frame will help most.

We believe communication should never be a barrier, so MaToS is free to start with no paywalls and no login required.

Open MaToS, start your camera and sign naturally. Computer vision reads your hands, face and body, and your translation appears as text as you go.

Your camera stays off until you start it, and your browser always asks for permission first. You can stop the camera at any time.
